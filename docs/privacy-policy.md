# Privacy Policy (DRAFT — requires legal review before launch)

_Last updated: DRAFT — not yet in force._

**Gurbani Guidance** is a free, non-commercial seva project that answers
questions from Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji.

## What we collect
- **Your questions are sensitive.** We treat question text as confidential.
  By default our servers log only an anonymous hash of each question — never
  the text itself — plus coarse metadata (timestamp, question category,
  answer latency).
- **No accounts, no tracking.** We do not require sign-in, set advertising
  cookies, or sell or share data with advertisers. Per-IP rate-limiting
  counters are held briefly for abuse prevention.

## AI processing
Questions are processed by third-party AI providers (Anthropic Claude and/or
Google Gemini **paid tiers**) solely to generate an answer. Under those
providers' commercial terms, your content is not used to train their models.
Cached answers are stored only for generic (non-personal) questions.

## Crisis content
If a question indicates a risk of self-harm we display crisis-helpline
information. We do not alert anyone or share the question.

## Retention
Operational logs (hashes + metadata) are retained at most 30 days.
